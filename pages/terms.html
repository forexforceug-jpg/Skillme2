6. Payments, wallet, and escrow

All payments are processed through Skillme2's wallet system. When you book a lesson, the lesson price is deducted from your available balance and held in escrow. When the lesson is completed and both parties confirm, the funds are released to the teacher minus the Platform's service fee.

Wallet balances are in USD. Top-ups are processed through third-party payment providers. Withdrawals are processed weekly to the teacher's chosen payout method.

The Platform's service fee is currently 20% of each lesson's price. This may change with notice.

7. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation refunds follow a sliding scale based on how close to the lesson you cancel:

  • 24+ hours before the lesson: 100% refund to your wallet

  • Less than 2 hours, or no-show: no refund
  • If the teacher cancels: 100% refund regardless of timing

Refunds are credited to your Skillme2 wallet, not your original payment method. Wallet funds can be used for future bookings.
