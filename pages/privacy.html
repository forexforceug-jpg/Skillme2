1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email, password hash (if you sign up with email), or Google user ID (if you sign in with Google).
  • Profile information: bio, avatar image URL, timezone, language, interests, and — if you become a teacher — specialty, rate, and payout method.
  • Lesson data: lessons you publish, bookings you make, sessions you complete.
  • Payment data: wallet balance, transaction history, and payout method details. We do not store full card numbers — payments are handled by our payment processors.
  • Messages: messages you send and receive on Skillme2.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and similar technical information collected by our hosting and analytics providers.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile and lessons to other users
  • Process bookings, payments, and refunds
  • Send transactional emails — booking confirmations, reminders, receipts, and important account notices
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms
  • Improve the Platform's features and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run ads targeted at you based on your profile or messages.

3. When we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Between student and teacher: when you book a lesson, the teacher sees your name, avatar, and any pre-lesson notes you provide. When a teacher publishes a lesson, students see their name, avatar, and lesson details.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and authentication), Resend (transactional email), Zoom (live lesson video), and our payment processors. These providers only see what is needed to perform their function.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, subpoena, or a valid request from a government authority.
  • Business transfers: if Skillme2 is acquired or merged, your information may transfer as part of that transaction.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Platform is used. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may prevent you from staying signed in.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and transmitted over HTTPS. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never visible to us.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that your data will never be compromised, but we work to protect it with reasonable technical and organizational measures.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — request a copy in a portable format
  • Correct inaccurate data — most of it you can edit directly in Settings
  • Delete your account and personal data — request deletion at any time
  • Object to certain processing — such as marketing emails
  • Export your lesson history and wallet transactions

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@skillme2.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Skillme2 is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 13, do not create an account. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date. If the changes are material, we'll notify you by email or by a prominent notice on the Platform.

Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@skillme2.com.
